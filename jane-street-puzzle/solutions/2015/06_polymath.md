# Polymath - June 2015 (Solution)

**Puzzle:** [06_polymath.md](../../puzzles/2015/06_polymath.md)

## Official Solution

As most solvers realized, it was better to try and use a large n with fewer n-ominoes than a smaller n with many n-ominoes.

The top score we received from a handful of solvers was **20,160**.

Interestingly, there are (at least) 2 different ways of getting to this score — one way is presented in the solution image, see if you can find the other!
