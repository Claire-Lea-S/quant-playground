# Hall of Mirrors - April 2015 (Solution)

**Puzzle:** [04_hall_of_mirrors.md](../../puzzles/2015/04_hall_of_mirrors.md)

## Official Solution

In this month's puzzle it was hard to know for sure if you had an optimal solution, but there were a few things to note:

1. There were more laser entrances (24) than exits (21), so not every laser can reach a target.

2. You get more points for a successful laser than you lose for a wall of length 1, so in general building mirrors is worthwhile, even if they're small.

3. There were some locations where placing one mirror caused 2 lasers on opposite ends to reach their goals.

The best solution we received (and the best we're aware of) totalled **77** points.
