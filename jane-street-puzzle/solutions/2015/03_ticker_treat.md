# Ticker Treat - March 2015 (Solution)

**Puzzle:** [03_ticker_treat.md](../../puzzles/2015/03_ticker_treat.md)

## Official Solution

As its title suggested, this month's puzzle had to do with stock tickers. Solving the 5 crossword-style clues leads to the names of 5 companies, with their stock tickers in parentheses:

1. They cause auroras (5,5) = Solar Winds (SWI)
2. Cross where it's not too deep (4) = Ford (F)
3. Cricket owner (2&1) = AT&T (T)
4. Ancient box-opener? (7) = Pandora (P)
5. Wheel part, to a shark? (3) = Ace (ACE)

That last one proved pretty tricky, but many of you were able to backsolve — the "shark" in question is a card shark, and a wheel is an A-2-3-4-5 straight.

Putting those stock tickers together reveals SWIFT PACE, a synonym for Sprint, whose stock ticker is **S**, the answer to this month's puzzle.
