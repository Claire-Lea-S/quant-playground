# What a Trit! - July 2020 (Solution)

**Puzzle:** [07_what_a_trit.md](../../puzzles/2020/07_what_a_trit.md)

## Official Solution

The puzzle is solvable, with multiple correct solutions. One example is:

**1TTTTTTTTT1TTTTTT00T0000011/111111111T0000001**

These trits, when converted to base 10, represent the ratio 3002408341848069/5726584833 = **524293**.

It turns out every odd integer can be written as a ratio of representable numbers, although the proof is far from trivial.
