# Candy Collectors - October 2020 (Solution)

**Puzzle:** [10_candy_collectors.md](../../puzzles/2020/10_candy_collectors.md)

## Official Solution

The most straightforward way to compute this was to assign a particular candy to each child and compute the probability of that (writing some code certainly helped here), then multiplying the result by 120 to account for the disjoint other assignments of candy to trick-or-treater.

**Answer: 318281087/8016470462**

This comes to just under 4%.
