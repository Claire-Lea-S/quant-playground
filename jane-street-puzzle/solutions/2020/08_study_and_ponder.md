# Study and Ponder - August 2020 (Solution)

**Puzzle:** [08_study_and_ponder.md](../../puzzles/2020/08_study_and_ponder.md)

## Official Solution

After **S**tudying **&** **P**ondering, solvers noticed that there were stock tickers among the jumble of letters. The "Aha!" was that the letters form a string containing all but one of the S&P 500 index members.

**Answer: SPGI**

The ticker of S&P Global Inc., the corporation responsible for the S&P 500 index itself - a satisfyingly "meta" solution.
