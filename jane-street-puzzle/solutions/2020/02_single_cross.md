# Single-Cross - February 2020 (Solution)

**Puzzle:** [02_single_cross.md](../../puzzles/2020/02_single_cross.md)

## Official Solution

**Answer: D = 1, probability = 2/π ≈ 0.6366**

The optimal length is D = 1, and the resulting probability is 2/π (about 0.6366).

This is related to Buffon's needle problem.
