# The Wright Stuff - November 2016 (Solution)

**Puzzle:** [11_the_wright_stuff.md](../../puzzles/2016/11_the_wright_stuff.md)

## Official Solution

**Answer: Kentuck Knob**

This was our toughest puzzle in a long time! To solve it, you needed to locate words in our word search. But of course very few words are present in the original grid.

"MINERAL" is present at the bottom of the grid, but there's not much else. However if you remove MINERAL from the grid and allow the rest of the letters to fall down to the line supporting the grid, the word "FOUNTAIN" also reveals itself.

If you proceed in this fashion you'll find many other words, all of which can complete a common two-word phrase with the word "water":
MINERAL, FOUNTAIN, BOTTLED, FALL, GROUND, PARK, SLIDE, TAP, SUPPLY, SALT, VAPOR, HOLY, FRESH, SKIING, BRACKISH, ICE, FOWL, HOT, SEA, BUFFALO, MAIN, POLO

When you're done, the letters left over in the grid anagram to "**Kentuck Knob**", a house designed by famous architect Frank Lloyd **Wright** (who also, of course, designed "Fallingwater").
