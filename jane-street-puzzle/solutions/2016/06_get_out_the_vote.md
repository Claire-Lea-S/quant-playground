# Get Out The Vote - June 2016 (Solution)

**Puzzle:** [06_get_out_the_vote.md](../../puzzles/2016/06_get_out_the_vote.md)

## Official Solution

The only way in which Harry could have lost a delegate in this fashion would be for the original vote counts to have been **(Harry, Larry, Mary) = (20, 34, 48)**.

Originally Harry's remainder is the highest of the 3, but after adding the extra delegate and his extra vote, Harry's becomes the lowest of the 3!
