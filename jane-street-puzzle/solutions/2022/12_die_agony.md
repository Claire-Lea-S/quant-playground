# Die Agony - December 2022 (Solution)

**Puzzle:** [12_die_agony.md](../../puzzles/2022/12_die_agony.md)

## Official Solution

### Die Labeling

The only labeling of the die that permits a legal route is: **−3, 9, 5, −9, 7, 9**

### Answer

The sum of the unvisited squares is **1935**.

### Note

After the 26th move, several entries claimed a path existed through the 452 immediately *north* of the 317. But moving to that cell would cause the score to increase by 27×9 = 243, which is not legal. The only legal move is to the eastern 452, as 452 = 317 + 27×5.
