# New York Minute - August 2022 (Solution)

**Puzzle:** [08_new_york_minute.md](../../puzzles/2022/08_new_york_minute.md)

## Official Solution

The puzzle master's to-do list is a word search with a punny twist! Famous NYC locations are scattered about the grid with substitutions to some letters:

d → c, c → m, m → μ, μ → n, n → p, p → f, f → a, a → z, z → y

The strange μ and references to small (or "minute") things throughout the text clued that this substitution was generated by moving one rung down the metric prefixes ladder: deci, centi, milli, micro, nano, pico, femto, atto, zepto, and yocto.

With that guide in hand, the 23 tasks could be crossed out, leaving "little itzly" left over, which is the substitution for the thematic area of Manhattan.

**Answer: Little Italy**
