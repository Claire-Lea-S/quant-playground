# Twenty Four Seven (Four-in-One) - February 2023 (Solution)

**Puzzle:** [02_twenty_four_seven_four_in_one.md](../../puzzles/2023/02_twenty_four_seven_four_in_one.md)

## Official Solution

### Answer

The empty regions of size greater than 1 have sizes 5, 3, 5, 8, 6, 4, 4, 9, 6, 3, 4, and 2, which multiply to **74,649,600**.
