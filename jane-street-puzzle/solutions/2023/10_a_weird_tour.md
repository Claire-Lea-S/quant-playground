# A Weird Tour - October 2023 (Solution)

**Puzzle:** [10_a_weird_tour.md](../../puzzles/2023/10_a_weird_tour.md)

## Official Solution

### The Pattern

The puzzle is about the five vowels in English. Key hints:
- Title mentions "Weird" (contains all vowels)
- 120 = 5! countries (permutations of vowels)
- "Gradually shifting dialects"
- Transport names contain pairs of vowels

### The Mechanism

Every answer has exactly one of each of the five vowels, and the order of these vowels is determined by:
1. Position along the journey
2. Transport method (contains the two consecutive vowels that get swapped)

Journey begins with AEIOU, ends with UOIEA.
