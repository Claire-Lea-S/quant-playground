# Hall of Mirrors 2 - December 2023 (Solution)

**Puzzle:** [12_hall_of_mirrors_2.md](../../puzzles/2023/12_hall_of_mirrors_2.md)

## Official Solution

### The Pattern

The path of light passes through each of the sixteen white 3-by-3 regions, through their centers, and at angles that are multiples of π/8.

This corresponds to **flag semaphore lettering**. (Weakly alluded to by the yellow and red coloring of the boundary cells.)

### Key Detail

The extra orange segment is an apostrophe, not a mirror.

### Answer

When translated, the message reads: **SEASON'S GREETINGS**
