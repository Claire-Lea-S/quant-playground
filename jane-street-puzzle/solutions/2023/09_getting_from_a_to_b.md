# Getting from a to b - September 2023 (Solution)

**Puzzle:** [09_getting_from_a_to_b.md](../../puzzles/2023/09_getting_from_a_to_b.md)

## Official Solution

### The Hidden Clue

The sentence that began "Initially, " and sounded like chatbot slurry was itself a major clue. The *initial* letters from the words that followed spell out:

**"n to the n equals a to the b"**

### The Pattern

The *n*th level of the tower displays the number of ways to rewrite nⁿ in the form aᵇ, where a and b can be positive or negative integers.

(The third paragraph hints at "negatives" and "positives".)

### The Answer

The least integer n for which nⁿ permits exactly 2023 such re-writings is:

**2² × 3¹⁶ × 5¹⁶ = 26,273,633,422,851,562,500**
