# Tile and Trouble 2 - February 2019 (Solution)

**Puzzle:** [02_tile_and_trouble_2.md](../../puzzles/2019/02_tile_and_trouble_2.md)

## Official Solution

The 100 scrabble tiles, when arranged in order into a 10-by-10 grid, reveal a knight's tour starting at the letters T, O, U, R for the first 90 steps.

There is only one way to complete the last 10 steps. Steps 96 through 99 (corresponding to the "????" in the original puzzle) spell the word:

**Answer: MOJO**
