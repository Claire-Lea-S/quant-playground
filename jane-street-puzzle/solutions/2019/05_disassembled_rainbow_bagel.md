# Disassembled Rainbow Bagel - May 2019 (Solution)

**Puzzle:** [05_disassembled_rainbow_bagel.md](../../puzzles/2019/05_disassembled_rainbow_bagel.md)

## Official Solution

**Answer: π − 1**

The loops can be dissected into overlapping rectangles, quarter-circles, and a central square. The diameters of the seven circles are 670, 671, 672, 673, 674, 675, 676.
