# Well Well Well... - June 2017 (Solution)

**Puzzle:** [06_well_well_well.md](../../puzzles/2017/06_well_well_well.md)

## Official Solution

The diagram shows the depths of the 49 sections after exactly 360 minutes have elapsed. After this moment, the water level will begin to rise on the bottom-right-most section.

**Answer: 360**
