# Crosswords - December 2017 (Solution)

**Puzzle:** [12_crosswords.md](../../puzzles/2017/12_crosswords.md)

## Official Solution

The arrows in the diagram define two loops, which intersect each other at the squares marked in the grid. In each orange rectangular region, the marked squares can be interpreted as a letter of the Braille alphabet.

Those letters spell out:

**Answer: NOVUS ANNUS** ("New Year" in Latin)
