# Turn-based Strategy Game - October 2017 (Solution)

**Puzzle:** [10_turn_based_strategy_game.md](../../puzzles/2017/10_turn_based_strategy_game.md)

## Official Solution

Rotate several of the octagonal tiles so that each group of similarly colored pegs is connected.

The minimal amount of rotation (counter-clockwise) needed for the tiles: Top row: 1, 0, 3 (one-eighth turns). Next five rows: 1,4,1; 1,7,1; 1,5,4; 1,6,5; 1,6,3.

When the values 103, 141, 171, 154, 165, 163 are converted to ASCII as base-8 integers, they yield the letters C, a, y, l, u, s — the diagram is ASCII-ing ("asking") the answer.

**Answer: Caylus** (a highly-rated strategy game)
