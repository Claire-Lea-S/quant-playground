# Hooks 11 - September 2025 (Solution)

**Puzzle:** [09_hooks_11.md](../../puzzles/2025/09_hooks_11.md)

## Official Solution

The image shows the completed grid, first with numbers assigned and second with pentomino letters labeled.

### Answer
The product of the empty regions is **1620**.

### Common Mistake
1296 was the most common incorrect answer submitted, which is what you get if you slide the I pentomino composed of 5's to the left one square. However, that arrangement leaves the filled squares in two disconnected regions, so it is not a valid arrangement.
