# Shut the Box - November 2025 (Solution)

**Puzzle:** [11_shut_the_box.md](../../puzzles/2025/11_shut_the_box.md)

## Official Solution

The diagram shows the cells that remain, which can be folded to form a 2×6×7 box. 

The product of the "sum of the numbered cells" values for the six faces is **16414860**.
