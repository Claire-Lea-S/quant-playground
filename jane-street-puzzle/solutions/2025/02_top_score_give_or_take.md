# Top Score (Give or Take) - February 2025 (Solution)

**Puzzle:** [02_top_score_give_or_take.md](../../puzzles/2025/02_top_score_give_or_take.md)

## Official Solution

### Finding the Pattern

The two word banks are both 14 words, and the colored numbers in the phrases are red and blue pairs of 1, 2,…, 14. Each number represents one of the word bank words, with top or bottom color coded.

### The Mechanism

The words can be considered as bags of letters. The "Give or Take" part of the title hints at anagramming and passing letters from top to bottom.

Solving reveals that the partial phrases are movie titles, and anagramming can create words like JURASSIC (from CUIRASS) or MINORITY (from TRIONYM).
