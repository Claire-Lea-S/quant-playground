# Some Ones, Somewhere - June 2025 (Solution)

**Puzzle:** [06_some_ones_somewhere.md](../../puzzles/2025/06_some_ones_somewhere.md)

## Official Solution

### The Pattern

Each picture represents an incomplete "partridge tiling" of a 45-by-45 square. The irregularly placed letters along the top and left edges become regular if you fill in the blank spaces along the boundary with the repeated alphabet string ABC…XYZABC…XYZABC…XYZABC…XYZABC…XYZABCDE.

### Extracting the Answer

Once you solve the nine grids and find where the 1-by-1 squares (the "ones") are located, you can extract a pair of letters (coordinates) for each of them.

For example, the "one" in the top-left picture is at the 19th row, 21st column, which maps to (S, U).

The coordinates for all nine grids:
- (S, U)
- (M, O)
- (F, C)
- (U, B)
- (E, S)
- (I, S)
- (S, Q)
- (U, A)
- (R, E)

Adding the spare tiles from the side tables in the first and seventh pictures gives the answer.

### Answer

**"The sum of cubes is a square"** — which is what the tilings prove!
