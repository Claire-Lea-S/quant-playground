# It's Symmetric 2 - July 2021 (Solution)

**Puzzle:** [07_its_symmetric_2.md](../../puzzles/2021/07_its_symmetric_2.md)

## Official Solution

The shaded region exhibits 180-degree symmetry about its center (which sneakily doesn't coincide with the center of the given grid).

**Answer: 503**

The sum of the squares of the areas of the connected unshaded regions comes to 503.
