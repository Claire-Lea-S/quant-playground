# Tit for Tat - March 2021 (Solution)

**Puzzle:** [03_tit_for_tat.md](../../puzzles/2021/03_tit_for_tat.md)

## Official Solution

The clue phrases followed by (X.Y) each had a two-word answer that consisted of words of length X that are identical except for the letter in position Y (hinted at in the puzzle's title, "Tit for Tat"):

| Clue | Answer |
|------|--------|
| Drivers destabilise? (6.4) | CAR**M**EN CAR**E**EN |
| Help a top-roper with your gut? (5.4) | BEL**L**Y BEL**A**Y |
| Weak point of a lever? (5.4) | CRA**N**K CRA**C**K |
| Reorganises puffy desserts? (8.2) | S**H**UFFLES S**O**UFFLES |
| What you need to get the flattest dough? (5.1) | **L**EAST **Y**EAST |
| Numerically, the body of water coming after the one at Edinburgh? (5.3) | FI**F**TH FI**R**TH |
| 2-for-1 on name-brand soap? (4.2) | D**I**AL D**E**AL |
| Nirvana feud? (6.4) | GRU**N**GE GRU**D**GE |

The differing letters spell out MELANCHOLYFRIEND, and we are left with "(6.1)" to suggest that this is a final clue.

**Answer: SOMBRE HOMBRE** (or MELLOW FELLOW)
