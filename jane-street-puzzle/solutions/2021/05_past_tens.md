# Past Tens - May 2021 (Solution)

**Puzzle:** [05_past_tens.md](../../puzzles/2021/05_past_tens.md)

## Official Solution

Most of the records in the collection are in alphabetical order, but not all. The records that are out of order are in positions 10, 11, 20, 21, 30, 31, …, 100, 101.

The records in every 10th spot have each received a "perfect 10" rating from Pitchfork.

The records "past" these (i.e., spots 11, 21, 31, …, 101) are:

- **What's** Going On (by Marvin Gaye)
- **The** Dark Side of the Moon (Pink Floyd)
- **Band** on the Run (Paul McCartney & Wings)
- **For** You (Tatsuro Yamashita)
- **The** Slider (T. Rex)
- **#1** Record (Big Star)
- **1973** (Placebo)
- **Song** Cycle (Van Dyke Parks)
- **My** Favorite Things (John Coltrane)
- **Love** is the Message (MFSB)

The first words form the question: "What's the band for the #1 1973 song 'My Love'?"

**Answer: Paul McCartney & Wings**
