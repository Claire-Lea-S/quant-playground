**Puzzle:** [07_many_happy_returns.md](../../puzzles/2024/07_many_happy_returns.md)

## Official Solution

### The Pattern

Each of the 20 recipients had something to *return*, literally. For example, that "important C++ component" was an *allocator*, which is "returning" the word *taco* (a popular type of street food). ("Allocator" has 9 letters and "taco" has 4, which is alluded to in the price $4.09.)

### Extracting the Final Answer

Taking the indicated letter of each recipient gives an extra clue: "…said the dairy producer". This is the person we need to match up the un-numbered 21st item (the good luck charms).

### Answer

**Cheesemonger** — who is returning some *gnomes*.
