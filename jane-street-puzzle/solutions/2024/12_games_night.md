# Games Night! - December 2024 (Solution)

**Puzzle:** [12_games_night.md](../../puzzles/2024/12_games_night.md)

## Official Solution

### Step 1: Finding the Letters

The game pieces, when grouped by the originating game, are arranged into letters. When reordering the letters with the game boxes at the top of the picture, the letters form "MISSING ONES".

### Step 2: Finding the Missing Pieces

Many (but not all) of the games are missing one piece (or type of piece):

| Game | Missing |
|------|---------|
| Uno | Yellow (of the four colors) |
| Catan | Ore (of the five resources) |
| Risk | Ural (of the Asian territories) |
| Monopoly | (complete) |
| Dominion | Silver (of the basic cards) |
| Scrabble | U tile |
| Sushi Go! | Nigiri cards |
| Chess | Kings |
| Clue | (complete) |
| Stratego | Miners |
| Bananagrams | Y tile |

Monopoly and Clue are the complete sets (Monopoly's completeness is confirmable by the eight game pieces printed on the side of the box).

### Extracting the Answer

Reading off the first letters of the missing ones, with spaces for the complete sets, gives:

**YOU SUNK MY**

This phrase is completed by the answer to form the tagline of another classic board game.

### Answer

**BATTLESHIP**
