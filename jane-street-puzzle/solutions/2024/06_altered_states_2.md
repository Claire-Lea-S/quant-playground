# Altered States 2 - June 2024 (Solution)

**Puzzle:** [06_altered_states_2.md](../../puzzles/2024/06_altered_states_2.md)

## Official Solution

Over 500 valid boards were received for this challenge.

### Statistics

- The most commonly used state was **Texas**, narrowly beating out Ohio and Iowa
- Connecticut and Rhode Island were the only states to appear in exactly zero boards
- The number of states appearing in valid boards ranged from 12 to 33

### Open Questions

- Does there exist a valid board that contains both Connecticut and Rhode Island, *and* excludes Texas?
- Does there exist a valid board with fewer than 12 states? Or more than 33?

### Notable Entries

Favorite boards came from: Alex Jian, Cathan Gormley, David Spencer, Mark Rothery, Josh Perks, and Daan Oorts.
