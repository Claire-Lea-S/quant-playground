# Number Cross 4 - May 2024 (Solution)

**Puzzle:** [05_number_cross_4.md](../../puzzles/2024/05_number_cross_4.md)

## Official Solution

### Answer

The sum of the concatenated numbers is **88,243,711,283**.

### Notable Feature

The top row, 11122233444, is both a square and has non-decreasing digits (see OEIS A028820).
