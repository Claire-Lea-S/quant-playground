# Fences 2 - September 2024 (Solution)

**Puzzle:** [09_fences_2.md](../../puzzles/2024/09_fences_2.md)

## Official Solution

The configuration of posts that permits a symmetric loop, passing through all six colored regions and making only 120-degree turns, is shown in the solution image.

### Answer

The product of the number of cells visited by the loop in each of the six regions is **404,320**.
