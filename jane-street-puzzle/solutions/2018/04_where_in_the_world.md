# Where in the World? - April 2018 (Solution)

**Puzzle:** [04_where_in_the_world.md](../../puzzles/2018/04_where_in_the_world.md)

## Official Solution

Each line can be unscrambled to spell out the name of a country, plus 1 other letter (or number). The residual characters spell out "search 4 directon".

This, along with the 4 highlighted "E"s, and the hint about finding the country "in the news" prompts looking for the letters N, E, W, and S (the 4 directions).

Using each E as a starting point, and traveling in the suggested direction until you hit another direction, spells out the answer.

**Answer: TOGO**
