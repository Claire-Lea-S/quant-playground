# Single-Cross - February 2020

**Category:** Geometry / Probability
**URL:** https://www.janestreet.com/puzzles/single-cross-index/

## Problem Statement

A random line segment of length D is chosen on a plane marked with an infinite checkerboard grid (i.e., a unit side length square grid).

**Question:** What length D maximizes the probability that the segment crosses exactly one line on the checkerboard grid, and what is this maximal probability?
