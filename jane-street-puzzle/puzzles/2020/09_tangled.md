# Tangled! - September 2020

**Category:** Mathematics / Group Theory
**URL:** https://www.janestreet.com/puzzles/tangled-index/

## Problem Statement

While on vacation in Terniquaternaria, Decimus got to experience the country's traditional folkloric dance, the *"Yawn-ock"*.

Four dancers hold one end of two long pieces of rope and never let go. Initial positions:

```
A D
\_________/
_________
/ \
B C
```

During the entire dance, only two types of move are allowed:
- **Rotate (R)**: The entire group rotates 90 degrees counterclockwise
- **Swap (S)**: Dancers in C and D swap places, with D holding the rope up while C passes under

From the initial configuration, a dance master calls out successive R/S steps randomly, that the dancers execute. The sequence was:

```
SRSRRSSRSRSSRSSRRSSRSSSSSRSSRSSRSRS
SRSSRSSSSSSSSRSSRSSSSSRSSRSSRRSSRSS
SSSRSSRSSRSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSRSSRSSRS
```

**Challenge:** What sequence would untangle the ropes?
