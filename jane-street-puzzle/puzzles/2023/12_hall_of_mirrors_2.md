# Hall of Mirrors 2 - December 2023

**Category:** Grid Puzzle / Visual
**URL:** https://www.janestreet.com/puzzles/hall-of-mirrors-2-index/

## Problem Statement

This puzzle is a callback to two older puzzles: the first "Hall of Mirrors" puzzle (bouncing rays of light off mirrors) and "Crosswords" (finding a message within a completed picture without explicit instructions).

The goal is to find a hidden message within the completed mirror grid.
