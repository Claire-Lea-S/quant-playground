# Number Cross 4 - May 2024

**Category:** Grid Puzzle
**URL:** https://www.janestreet.com/puzzles/number-cross-4-index/

## Problem Statement

The 11-by-11 grid has been divided into various regions. Shade some of the cells black, then place digits (0-9) into the remaining cells.

### Rules

- Shading must be "sparse": no two shaded cells may share an edge
- Every cell within a region must contain the same digit
- Orthogonally adjacent cells in different regions must have different digits
- Note: shading cells may break up regions or change which pairs of regions are adjacent
- Each row has been supplied with a clue
- *Every* number formed by concatenating consecutive groups of unshaded cells within a row must satisfy the clue given for the row
- Numbers must be at least **two digits long** and may not begin with a 0

**Answer:** The sum of all the numbers formed in the completed grid.
